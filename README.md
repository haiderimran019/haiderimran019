## Research

### [GPT-2 Small Mechanistic Interpretability](https://github.com/haiderimran019/gpt2-ioi-mechinterp)

A first hands-on research project investigating how GPT-2 Small performs the **Indirect Object Identification (IOI)** task.

The project uses activation patching, zero-ablation, attention-head analysis, MLP interventions, and robustness testing across different prompt conditions.

The project also includes a literature review covering circuit discovery and mechanistic interpretability, including work by Wang et al. and Conmy et al.

**Current stage:** Developing a focused research question based on the initial experiments and literature review.
